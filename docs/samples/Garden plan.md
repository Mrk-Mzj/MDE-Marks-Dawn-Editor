# Garden plan 2027

A small vegetable garden behind the house: four raised beds, a herb corner and a compost bin. The goal for this year is **fresh salad from May to October** and enough tomatoes to make sauce for the winter.

## Beds

| Bed | Size | Spring | Summer | Autumn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North | 2 × 1 m | Peas | Beans | Garlic |
| East | 2 × 1 m | Lettuce, radish | Cucumbers | Spinach |
| South | 3 × 1 m | Onions | Tomatoes | Green manure |
| West | 2 × 1 m | Carrots | Courgettes | Kale |

## Notes

> Water early in the morning, not in the evening: wet leaves overnight invite mildew.

Tomatoes need *at least six hours* of sun a day. Pinch out the side shoots every week and tie the stems as they grow. Keep the old variety from last year, it was the sweetest one.
