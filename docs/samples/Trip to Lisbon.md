# Trip to Lisbon

Five days in May, two adults, one carry-on each. We stay in Alfama and walk as much as we can.

## Plan

| Day | Morning | Afternoon | Evening |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thu | Arrival, check-in | Castle of São Jorge | Fado in Alfama |
| Fri | Tram 28 | Baixa and Chiado | Dinner at the market |
| Sat | Belém tower | Pastéis de nata | Sunset at a miradouro |
| Sun | Day trip to Sintra | Pena Palace | Back by train |
| Mon | Last coffee | Flight home | — |

## Packing list

- [x] Passports
- [x] Train tickets to Sintra
- [ ] Comfortable shoes (the hills!)
- [ ] Power adapter
- [ ] Light rain jacket

## Budget

- Flights: **240 €**
- Flat: **410 €**
- Food and transport: about **300 €**

> Tip: buy a Viva Viagem card at the airport; it works on the metro, trams and the train to Sintra.
